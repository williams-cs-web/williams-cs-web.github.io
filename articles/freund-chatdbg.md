Computer Science Professor **Stephen Freund** and his collaborators, Kyla Levin, Nicholas van Kempen, and Professor Emery Berger at the UMass Manning School of Computer Science were awarded a **Distinguished Artifact Award** at this year’s **FSE 2025** conference in Trondheim, Norway.  The Distinguished Artifact Award recognizes that the research software accompanying their paper, **ChatDBG: Augmenting Debugging with Large Language Models**, far exceeded the basic expectations of being available, functional, and reusable.  FSE is a prestiguous conference for software engineering, and Distinguished Artifact Awards are typically only awarded to a handful of authors each year.

You can read more about [their ChatDBG work and the award here](https://www.cics.umass.edu/news/2025-distinguished-artifact-award).
