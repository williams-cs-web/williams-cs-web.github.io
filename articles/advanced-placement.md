Students who are interested in pursuing a computer science degree typically begin with the first course of our introductory sequence (**CSCI 134: Introduction to Computer Science**).  However, under some circumstances, students are permitted to directly enroll in the second course of the sequence (**CSCI 136: Data Structures and Advanced Programming**):

- Students who have earned a 4 or higher on the AP Computer Science A Exam are automatically permitted to directly enroll in CSCI 136.
- Students who have not taken the AP Test, but feel that they have sufficient experience to directly enroll in CSCI 136, should contact our Placement Advisor (**Professor James Bern, jmb15@williams.edu)**.
