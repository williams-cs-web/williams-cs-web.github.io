These are the major requirements that applied to students who declared the computer science major before Spring 2026, when the department updated its requirements. If you declared prior to Spring 2026, the requirements below apply to you rather than our [current requirements](/plan-your-major/).

### Required Courses in Computer Science

**Introductory courses**

* CSCI 134: Introduction to Computer Science
* CSCI 136: Data Structures and Advanced Programming (placement into CSCI 136 required)

**Core courses**

* CSCI 237: Computer Organization
* CSCI 256: Algorithm Design and Analysis
* At least one of CSCI 334 (Principles of Programming Languages) or CSCI 361 (Theory of Computation)

**Electives**

Three or more electives chosen from 300/400-level Computer Science courses, bringing the total number of CS courses to at least eight. Courses with a 9 as the middle digit (reading courses, research, and thesis) normally do not count toward this requirement.

### Required Courses in Mathematics

Any Math or Stat course at the 200-level or higher, except MATH 200.

### Discrete Math Proficiency

Students must demonstrate proficiency in discrete mathematics, by the end of the sophomore year, either by earning a grade of C- or better in MATH 200, MATH 328, or MATH 341, or by passing the (now-discontinued) Discrete Math Proficiency Exam. The exam was slated for discontinuation in Fall 2025.

### Colloquium

All computer science majors must attend a total of at least twenty sessions of the Computer Science Colloquium. Juniors and seniors are encouraged to attend at least five sessions during each semester they are present on campus. A junior or senior studying away on an approved program receives four colloquium credits for each semester away, up to a total of eight credits.
